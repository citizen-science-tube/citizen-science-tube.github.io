Privacy Policy

Citizen Science Uploader · Last updated: October 17, 2026

About this application

Citizen Science Uploader is a private, single-user tool operated by the owner of the Citizen Science YouTube channel. Its only purpose is to upload and schedule videos to that channel. It is not available to the public.

Use of YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information handled through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Information accessed

With the owner's authorization, the application uses the YouTube upload permission to send video files and their metadata (title, description, tags, privacy and scheduling settings) to the channel owner's YouTube account. It does not read, collect or store data about any other user, viewer or channel.

How information is stored

The application stores only the OAuth access credentials needed to upload on the owner's behalf and the IDs of videos it has uploaded, on a private server controlled by the channel owner. No information is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Stored credentials are deleted when access is revoked or the application is no longer used.
