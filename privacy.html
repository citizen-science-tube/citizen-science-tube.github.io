Privacy Policy – Citizen Science Uploader

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Citizen Science Uploader ("the app", "we") accesses, uses, stores, shares and deletes data, including data obtained from Google APIs. The app is operated by the owner of the Citizen Science YouTube channel. Contact: citizen.science.tube@gmail.com.

1. What the app is

Citizen Science Uploader is a single-user tool that uploads and schedules educational videos to the Citizen Science YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner and does not serve other users.

2. Google user data we access

The app requests one OAuth scope:

ScopeWhy we need it
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload To upload video files to the owner's YouTube channel and set their metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy status, scheduled publish time).

The app does not read your Gmail, contacts, Drive files, watch history, subscriptions, comments, or any data about other YouTube users, viewers or channels.

3. Data we collect and store

  • OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) issued by Google, so the app can upload on the owner's behalf.
  • IDs of uploaded videos and their scheduling status, so the app can track what was published.
  • Video files and metadata prepared by the owner, held only temporarily during upload.

We do not collect names, email addresses, location, payment information, or analytics/tracking data. The app uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

4. How we use the data

Data is used only to provide the app's single feature: uploading and scheduling videos on the Citizen Science channel. It is not used for advertising, profiling, market research, or to train AI or machine-learning models.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, transfer or share Google user data with any third party. Video files and metadata are sent only to Google/YouTube through the YouTube Data API, as required to perform the upload. Data may be disclosed only if required by law.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Citizen Science Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Storage and security

OAuth tokens and video IDs are stored on a private server controlled by the channel owner, with access restricted to the owner. Connections to Google use HTTPS/TLS. Tokens are never published or shared.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Temporary video files are deleted after the upload finishes.
  • OAuth tokens are kept only while the app is in use and are deleted immediately when access is revoked or the app is retired.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To request deletion of any stored data, email citizen.science.tube@gmail.com; we will delete it within 30 days.

9. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

10. Prohibited content

The app uploads only original educational content. It is never used to create, generate or distribute sexually explicit or intimate imagery, including AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery.

11. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: citizen.science.tube@gmail.com
