Citizen Science Uploader

The publishing tool behind the Citizen Science YouTube channel.

What is Citizen Science Uploader?

Citizen Science Uploader is a small web application that uploads and schedules videos to the Citizen Science YouTube channel. Citizen Science is an educational channel that explains science, history and technology in a clear, friendly way.

What the app does

  • Uploads finished episode video files to the Citizen Science YouTube channel.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy status and publish date.
  • Schedules videos so they go public at a planned time.

Who can use it

The app is used only by the owner of the Citizen Science channel to manage that channel. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not access any other user's YouTube channel or data. This page is public and needs no login.

How it uses Google data

The app signs in with Google OAuth and requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope, which it uses solely to upload videos and their metadata to the owner's channel. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Content policy

Citizen Science Uploader publishes only original educational videos made for the Citizen Science channel. The app does not create, edit, generate or upload sexually explicit or intimate imagery of any kind, including AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII), and it does not use Google APIs for that purpose.

YouTube API Services

Citizen Science Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.
